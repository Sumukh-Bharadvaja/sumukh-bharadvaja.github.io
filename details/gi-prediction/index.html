Gastro‑Intestinal Disease Prediction

Data Science | KVASIR dataset • CNNs (VGG/ResNet/DenseNet/EfficientNet)

PythonTensorFlow/KerasCNN

Summary

Built image classifiers on the KVASIR endoscopy dataset to detect 8 GI disease classes. We benchmarked six architectures and report accuracy, precision, recall, and 5‑fold mean cross‑validation accuracy. DenseNet121 was best with ≈90.1% test accuracy and balanced precision/recall.
